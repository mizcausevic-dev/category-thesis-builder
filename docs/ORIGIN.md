# Origin

Category Thesis Builder exists after the scorecards, board brief, diligence pack, vertical brief, governance registry, deal radar, timing signal index, partner graph, exit room, and boardroom-sparring layer.

The goal is to turn that executive-intelligence estate into one explicit category-narrative surface:

- what category Kinetic Gain should claim first
- which themes are strong enough to anchor the lead story
- where the why-now case is still thin
- what should stay out of the investor memo until the evidence catches up
